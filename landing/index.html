Features

Everything a landlord tracks, without the spreadsheet.

Built around how renting works in the UAE: annual contracts, post-dated cheques, Ejari, DEWA and building service charges.

Cheque tracking and deposit reminders

Log each post-dated cheque once. Get a reminder 3 days and 1 day before it's due, and see what's pending, deposited, cleared, on hold or bounced.

Rent agreements and Ejari

Each unit's agreement, Ejari number, term and payment schedule in one record, with renewal alerts at 90, 60 and 30 days.

Scan documents to fill forms

Scan an Ejari certificate, tenancy contract, DEWA bill or title deed and LedGix Rent fills in the details for you to check.

In the iPhone and Android apps

Maintenance with quotes

Forward a request to your maintenance company, get their quote back and approve it, with every step kept on the ticket.

Utility bills and service charges

Keep DEWA bills and Mollak service charges with each unit, so nothing slips through between tenancies.

Reports

Collected and outstanding rent by month and by year, for one property or your whole portfolio.

Documents in one place

Title deeds, Emirates IDs, passports, contracts and bills stored privately with the property or tenant they belong to.

Arabic and English

Every screen works in both languages, right-to-left included. Switch any time.

FAQ

Questions landlords ask

Who is LedGix Rent for?
Landlords in the UAE who look after their own units, whether that's one apartment or a portfolio of apartments, villas and offices.
Does it work in Arabic?
Yes. The whole app is available in Arabic and English, with full right-to-left layout. You can switch language at any time.
Can it read my Ejari, tenancy contract or DEWA bill?
In the iPhone and Android apps, yes. Scan or upload the document and LedGix Rent fills in the details from its English text for you to review. Text that only appears in Arabic isn't read yet, and you can edit every field.
Is it connected to Ejari, DEWA or Mollak?
Not directly yet. LedGix Rent stores your Ejari, DEWA and Mollak details and documents alongside each unit. Direct connections to those services aren't switched on.
What is LedGix ID?
One account for every LedGix app. If you already use another LedGix app, sign in to LedGix Rent with the same LedGix ID. You can also use Google, Apple or email.
When are the iPhone and Android apps coming?
They're in testing now. Until they're out, you can use LedGix Rent in any browser, and your data will be there when you install the app.
How do I delete my account?
Choose Delete account in the app's settings. It permanently removes your account, records and files. If you can't get into the app, email support@getledgix.com from your account's email address.
How do I get help?
Email us at support@getledgix.com.
